Quick rules primer

You're about to play two short Othello (also called Reversi) endgame puzzles. You'll have 8 or 1.5 minutes per puzzle to play the position out to the end.

Othello rules

  • Players take turns placing one disc of their color on an empty square.
  • A move is only legal if it traps a straight line of the opponent's discs between the disc you just played and another of your own.
  • Every trapped disc flips to opposite color.
  • The game ends when neither player can move. Whoever has more discs on the board wins.

An AI assistant may be available to help you think through your moves.
