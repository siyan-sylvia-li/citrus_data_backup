Game Study

Informed Consent

About this study

This is a research study on how proficient AI users make decisions while playing AI-assisted games, so that we can help less-experienced users on similar tasks. Your goal is to play three {{ topic }} puzzles.

What you will do (~16 minutes)

  1. A prompt-writing task (~2 minutes).
  2. IF YOUR CREATED PROMPT IS HIGH-QUALITY: Play the first {{ topic }} game (8 minutes), with AI assistance.
  3. Reflection questions (~2 minutes).
  4. Play a round of two games (3 minutes), without AI assistance.
  5. Reflection questions (~1 minute).

Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw any time.

Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to participate. You must not be a skilled Othello player.

Risks & discomforts

Participation in this study involves minimal risk.

Benefits

There are no direct benefits to you from participating beyond the listed compensation.

Confidentiality & data retention

We collect your Prolific ID, the prompt you create, demographics, your conversation with the AI, and the moves you make during the game. Data is stored securely and used for research only; de-identified results may be published.

Questions or concerns

This study is conducted by Siyan Li, at Together AI. If you have questions about the study, contact siyanli@together.ai.
