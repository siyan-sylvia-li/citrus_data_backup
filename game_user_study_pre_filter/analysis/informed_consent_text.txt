# Informed Consent

## About this study

This is a research study on how proficient AI users make
decisions while playing AI-assisted games, so that we can
help less-experienced users on similar tasks.
Your goal is to complete a
Connect Four puzzle.

## What you will do (~10 minutes)

- A prompt-writing task (~2 minutes).
- IF YOUR CREATED PROMPT IS HIGH-QUALITY: Play the Connect Four puzzle (6 minutes), with AI assistance available.
- Reflection questions (~2 minutes).

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw any time.

## Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to participate. You must not be a skilled Connect Four player.

## Risks & discomforts

Participation in this study involves minimal risk.

## Benefits

There are no direct benefits to you from participating beyond the
listed compensation.

## Confidentiality & data retention

We collect your Prolific ID, the prompt you write for the warm-up task, demographics, your chat transcript with
the AI, and the moves you make during the puzzle. Data
is stored securely and used for research only; de-identified results
may be published.

## Questions or concerns

This study is conducted by Siyan Li, at Together AI. If you have
questions about the study, contact siyanli@together.ai.
