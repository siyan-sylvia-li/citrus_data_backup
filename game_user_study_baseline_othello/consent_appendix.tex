\textbf{About this study.}
This is a research study on how \textbf{users with different AI usage skills} make
decisions while playing games. Your goal is to play three \textbf{Othello} puzzles.

\textbf{What you will do ($\sim$12 minutes).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item A \textbf{prompt-writing task ($\sim$2 minutes)}.
  \item IF YOUR CREATED PROMPT IS HIGH-QUALITY: Play the first \textbf{Othello} game (5 minutes).
  \item \textbf{Reflection questions} ($\sim$1 minute).
  \item Play a round of two games (3 minutes).
  \item \textbf{Reflection questions} ($\sim$1 minute).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Voluntary participation.}
Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw any time.

\textbf{Eligibility.}
You must be 18 years of age or older to participate. You must not be a skilled
Othello player.

\textbf{Risks \& discomforts.}
Participation in this study involves minimal risk.

\textbf{Benefits.}
There are no direct benefits to you from participating beyond the listed
compensation.

\textbf{Confidentiality \& data retention.}
We collect your Prolific ID, the prompt you create, demographics, and the moves
you make during the game. Data is stored securely and used for research only;
de-identified results may be published.

\textbf{Questions or concerns.}
This study is conducted by \textsc{[Author]}, at \textsc{[Institution]}. If you have
questions about the study, contact \textsc{[email]}.
