Quick rules primer

You're about to play two short Connect Four tasks. You'll have 3 or 6 minutes per game to decide on the best first three moves for Red — the moves that keep Red on track to force a win.

Connect 4 Rules

Two players take turns dropping colored discs into columns. The disc falls to the lowest empty space in that column. The first player to connect four of their discs in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.

An AI assistant may be available on the game screen to help you think through your moves.
